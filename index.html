04 — Doctrine

How the work ships

I

Open source by default

Tools should be forkable, inspectable, and free to improve. No black boxes unless the mission demands it.

II

Local-first privacy

Your machine is the fortress. Data stays local unless the user explicitly opens the gate.

III

Ship working steel

Demos are nice. Builds are better. Every project should leave behind something real, runnable, and hardened.
